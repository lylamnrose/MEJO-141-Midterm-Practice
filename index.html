Professionalism: six criteria and ombuds
  1. Specialized knowledge: training, accreditation (optional), licensing (required)
  2. Public service over self service
  3. Associations (individuals join, not companies)
  4. Self-regulation, enforceable codes (enforceable = you lose the ability to practice; none in communication)
  5. Status (public trust)
  6. Autonomy
  • Purists: all six. Others: a majority. Some but not all: semi-profession.
  • Ombuds / public editor: independent boundary spanner between newsroom and public; investigates complaints, recommends, cannot change policy.
